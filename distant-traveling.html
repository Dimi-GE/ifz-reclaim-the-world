Reclaim The World Distant Traveling

Module · by Dimi-GE

  1. Opt-in per migration One checkbox under "Zone cleared". Unticked, the convoy works as usual.
  2. Pick any place The game's own globe: search a city or click anywhere.
  3. A real migration The convoy arrives as usual; Supply Lines keeps working.
  4. Special zones Raiders, farmers, military and more, made by the game's own rules around far picks.
  5. Zone download Asks first, shows the count. No network: back to the globe, pick again to retry.
  6. BACK to the globe From the tile view; "change area" in the new game opens the globe too.
