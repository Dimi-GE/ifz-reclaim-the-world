Reclaim The World Supply Lines

Module · by Dimi-GE

  1. Departure record When the convoy leaves, the settlement is saved with what it made, used and eats.
  2. Claim on arrival At the first save of the new settlement, the settlement the convoy came from is found.
  3. Sunrise shipments and penalties Surplus is added to the stock, shortfalls are taken from it, netted across all old settlements.
  4. Overflow carried over What does not fit arrives at the next sunrise.
  5. Chain After another migration, the earlier settlements keep supplying.
  6. Safe switch WriteStock = false logs only and changes nothing.
  7. Pager In Settlement Panel: page through the old settlements.
